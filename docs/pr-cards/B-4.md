## Built

- `src/github/prices.js`: price table with source and date, whole-cent calculators, `priceLine`.
- `src/tools/spend-guard.js`: `denverMonthStart`, `spendStatus`, `spendGate` (refuse at $0 with how to raise; refuse over the limit naming used and remaining; otherwise confirm; refuse raising through the tool).
- `src/tools/people-guard.js`: own-people set, mentions, `notifyOutsiders`, access helpers, `peopleGate` (a tool declared as widening access always asks). Gates now: production, protected resources, people, spend.
- `github_set_spend_limit` (lower only; profiles everyday and full).
- `src/db.js` and fake: `setSpendLimitCents`, `sumSpendCents`.
- Setup page: "Monthly spend limit" (dollars, shows used this month, logged) and "Your own people" (extra names, logged).
- Tests: prices (4), spend-guard (13), people-guard (11), setup-page (3 new), spend contract on fake and Postgres.
