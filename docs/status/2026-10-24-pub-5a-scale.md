# 2026-10-24 — PUB-5 PR A: works for thousands of people

**What now works:** three jobs that used to open every person's store one by one (and stop at 100 or 1,000 people) no longer do:
- **App uninstall clean-up** finds the right people with one lookup (new table `gh_public_installations`: numbers only, no names, no tokens) and opens only their stores. With 500+ people present it took about 3 ms and touched only the matching person.
- **Delete a person** finds them through their saved sign-in (one query) and still confirms inside their own store; if that sign-in was already removed it scans everyone in pages of 200. No 100 ceiling.
- **Owner usage summary** reads people in pages of 200, no ceiling, and says `partial: true` (with how many it did count) if it runs past 8 seconds, never a wrong total.

**People who signed in before this change:** the first time an uninstall finds nobody, the lookup is filled once from each person's own store and a marker is saved (two at once is safe). The lookup is also emptied when a person is deleted (`gh_forget_public_tenant`).

**How it was checked:** `tests/db/pub-5a-scale.test.js` (real throwaway Postgres, 6 tests) and one sign-in test added to `tests/db/pub-6-public-connect.test.js`. Local load test, fake GitHub: 500 people signed in and made 5 calls each, 25 at a time, 0 errors, 114 s total (sign-in p95 5.2 s, whoami p95 2.1 s on this small machine).

**Owner action:** none. **Next:** PR B (fair use, cost auto-pause, no people cap).
