**Merged** as PR #341 (merge commit 1d856fb; CI `ci` and `release-policy` green at head 9a949c7). One review finding fixed before merge: key listing now chooses the credential per key kind.   invitations, follow, watch, star, star lists, social links, status, your Codespaces, organization migrations) now use
  your master token when you added one and it carries the right permission. Without one, or on a read-only connection or

  tick on GitHub to unlock it). The master-token page has a second link that adds the optional enterprise permissions.
  Code: `src/github/master-areas.js`.
